Handcrafted Warmth & Heritage

A Cozy Sanctuary in
Nuwara Eliya’s Hills

Step away from the chilly mountain mist into our warmly lit dining haven. At The Rustique Restaurant, every detail is intentional—from custom slatted timber partitions to exposed brick walls that radiate rustic character.

Our dining space is adorned with woven rattan pendant lamps casting soft radial light patterns, fine lace tablecloths, and mirrored wooden shelves. We blend Sri Lankan culinary soul with global gourmet techniques for an unforgettable dining experience.
